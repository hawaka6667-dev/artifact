# Third-party data and art

- Constellation illustrations: Stellarium "modern" sky culture, Free Art License 1.3 (https://artlibre.org/licence/lal/en/). Credit shown in the page.
- Constellation boundaries (IAU): from d3-celestial (https://github.com/ofrohn/d3-celestial), BSD-3-Clause; boundary data originates from the IAU / Delporte.
- Anchor-star positions, brightest-star and naked-eye-star counts: HYG database (https://github.com/astronexus/HYG-Database), CC BY-SA 4.0.
- Sky culture metadata (names, anchors): Stellarium, CC BY-SA 4.0.
- Constellation areas are computed from the boundary data.
